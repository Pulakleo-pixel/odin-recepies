"Chicken kassa"

"Description"

The chicken kassais a delicious and spicy Odia-style chicken dish cooked with aromatic spices. It features tender chicken pieces coated in a thick, rich onion-tomato masala. The perfect blend of ginger, garlic, and traditional spices gives it a unique flavour. Chicken Kassa tastes best when served hot with steamed rice, roti, or paratha.

"Ingredients"

  • Chicken
  • potatoes
  • Tomatoes
  • Ginger garlic paste

"Steps"

  1. Marinate the chicken: Mix chicken with turmeric, red chilli powder, salt, ginger-garlic paste, and curd. Leave for 30 minutes.
  2. Fry the onions: Heat mustard oil in a kadai and fry sliced onions until golden brown. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chillies.
  3. Prepare the masala: Add chopped tomatoes, turmeric, chilli powder, coriander powder, and cumin powder. Cook until the masala thickens and oil separates.
  4. Cook the chicken: Add the marinated chicken and sauté for 8–10 minutes. Cover and cook on low heat for 25–35 minutes, stirring occasionally, until fully cooked. Add a little hot water if needed.
  5. Finish and serve: Add garam masala and freshly chopped coriander leaves. Cook for another 2 minutes and serve hot with steamed rice, roti, or paratha.
  6. For authentic Odia-style Chicken Kassa, use mustard oil and cook the masala slowly until it becomes thick and richly coated around the chicken.
